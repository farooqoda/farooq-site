Most businesses don't have a lead problem. They have a follow-up problem.

Someone calls while you're with a customer. Someone messages at 9pm. Someone asks a question on Tuesday and hears nothing until Friday. I build the system that answers every one of them in under a minute — then keeps following up until they book or tell you no.

01 — The gap
After hours
Most enquiries arrive when nobody is at the desk. They don't wait until morning — they call the next name on the list.
The 4th message
Almost every tool sends the first reply. The booking usually comes from the third or fourth follow-up, which nobody sends.
No-shows
A booking with no reminder before it is a coin toss. The slot is gone and the lead is gone with it.

None of this is a staffing failure. It's what happens when the person answering enquiries is also the person doing the work.

02 — What I build

One system, every channel your customers already use.

Phone, text, WhatsApp, email, your website, Instagram and Facebook — all landing in one place, with one shared history per person. It answers in their words, on their channel, and books straight into your real calendar.

Then the part almost nobody does: it remembers. Reminders before the appointment. A nudge if someone goes quiet. A recap after a call. A review request afterwards. It stops the moment they reply.

03 — How it runs
01

It picks up, whatever the channel

A missed call, a text, a WhatsApp message, a form on your site, an Instagram DM. Same system, same knowledge, one conversation history per person.

02

It answers from what you told it

Your hours, your pricing, your policies, your services. It never invents an answer — anything it doesn't know goes to you instead of being guessed at.

03

It books against your real calendar

It only offers times that are genuinely free, writes the appointment into your calendar, and handles reschedules without you being involved.

04

It reminds before the appointment

A message the day before and an hour before. If they say they can't make it, it moves them rather than losing them.

05

It follows up when someone goes quiet

Up to three spaced messages referencing what they actually asked about. It stops the instant they reply, and never sends a fourth.

06

It hands you anything sensitive

Complaints, unusual requests, anything outside what it knows — straight to your phone with the full conversation attached.

04 — You see all of it

Every action it took, and every one it deliberately didn't.

You get a dashboard and a short summary each morning. Not vanity numbers — the actual log: what was scheduled, what was sent, what was called off and why.

It also tells you what it couldn't answer. Once a week you get the questions that stumped it, grouped together. You write the answers, and it knows them permanently. The system gets better because your customers keep asking.

05 — What it won't do

It won't spam anyone

Three follow-ups maximum, ever. It stops the moment someone replies, and it will not message anyone who hasn't consented to be contacted — the system refuses, rather than asking you to remember.

It won't pretend to be human

It's helpful and it sounds like your business, but it doesn't claim to be a person. When someone needs a human, it gets them one instead of stalling.

It won't make things up

It answers from what you've told it and nothing else. A question outside that goes to you — and then into the weekly list of things worth teaching it.
